About SkillSwap

SkillSwap is a student-to-student skill-sharing platform where students exchange their knowledge and learn new skills without spending money.

Project Objective

To connect students with different skills and encourage knowledge sharing and teamwork.

Benefits

  • Free knowledge sharing.
  • Improves communication.
  • Builds confidence.
  • Develops technical skills.
  • Encourages teamwork.

Future Enhancements

AI skill matching, online chat, video sessions, notifications and student feedback.
